Cedi Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Cedi Ledger is an invoicing and expense app for small businesses and their teams. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you choose, your name, job title, phone number and profile photo.
  • From Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access anything else in your Google account.
  • Business records you enter: invoices, receipts, expenses, budgets, client details, your business details and logo.

How it is used

This information is used only to run the app: to sign you in, show your records to you and your team, and produce invoices and receipts. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside your team.

Who can see your information

Records belong to a business. Only people that business has invited can see them, and owners and admins control who is invited. Your name, job title and photo are visible to people on the same team.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and a copy is kept on your device so the app works offline. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile or remove your photo at any time in Settings.
  • Save a copy of your records from Settings → Data & backup.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Security. This removes your login and profile, and any business you own on your own.
